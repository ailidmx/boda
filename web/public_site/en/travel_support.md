# I Am Coming From Far Away

This section is for guests traveling from abroad, especially from France.

## Special support
- Pre-trip help for flight booking.
- Route and timing recommendations.
- Airport pickup coordination.
- Ongoing support during the full stay (Friday to Sunday).

## What we will do for you
- Help you choose flights aligned with event timing.
- Share safe transfer options.
- Provide a fast WhatsApp contact point.

## Information we need from you
- Arrival airport.
- Flight number.
- Estimated arrival time.
- Any special transport needs.
